What happens

About three minutes, shown step by step on the job page. Production comes later, with Promote to prod.

  1. RepositoryCreates the public repository {{ owner }}/<name> from the stack's template.
  2. First buildChecks the app and publishes ghcr.io/{{ owner }}/<name>.
  3. Pull requestAdds <name>/staging here, set up from the app's swhurl.yaml and what you chose under Connects to: the apps it calls start admitting it, and it gets a key for its events.
  4. After you mergeEvery push to the app's main deploys to staging.
